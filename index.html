Function & Event Orders

Planning a wedding, birthday, house-warming, office party, or family function? We take bulk orders and event catering — from biryani buckets to full meal spreads, cooked fresh in Amma's home style.

  • Bulk biryani orders (chicken / mutton / egg)
  • Full meals & buffet spreads for gatherings
  • Chettinad & tandoor party platters
  • Custom menus for your event & budget
